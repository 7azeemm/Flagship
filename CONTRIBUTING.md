# Contributing

FlagShip is no longer actively maintained. Forks and independent experiments are welcome, but there is no commitment to respond to issues, review pull requests, or publish updates.

If you work on a fork:

1. Read the [setup guide](docs/setup.md) before making changes.
2. Keep credentials, personal data, retailer exports, and local databases out of commits.
3. Check retailer terms, robots/access policies, and applicable law before running scrapers.
4. Keep retailer-specific extraction isolated in its adapter and avoid changing catalog semantics without explaining data impacts.
5. Include a concise description and manual verification notes with proposed changes.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it is offered under the repository's MIT license. You retain ownership of your contribution. Third-party assets and dependencies remain under their own licenses.
